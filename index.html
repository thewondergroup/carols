To your customer, the answer is the product

To your customer, the answer is the product

Your customer never sees the work. Not the website rebuild, not the reviews you chased, not the directory listings your team kept up to date for years.

They see one answer in a chat. That answer is the entire surface of all that work. If you’re not in it, none of it happened.

All of it depends on the part you control least. You own the website, but the assistant decides who gets named.

Checking by hand doesn’t scale, so most businesses never look, or ask once and guess. Neither gets you recommended.

Deep research

Wonderscore reads your website the way an assistant does: services, locations, prices, reviews. Then it writes twenty questions real customers actually ask.

Mention check

Each question goes to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. We record who got named, who got cited, and whether you were anywhere in the answer.

The plan

You get a score and a weekly plan. Every item is one specific change on a specific page,
with the reason an assistant would care.

Automation

Built to keep tracking without anyone maintaining it. Wonderscore re-runs the questions every week, scores what changed, and refreshes the plan.

  • Wonderscore watches the assistants your customers actually use. Results land in your dashboard and a weekly email; the plan is yours to hand to whoever runs your site.

  • Every Monday: your score, what moved, and the three fixes that matter most. Reply to it and it reaches a person.

  • Share the dashboard with your web developer or agency. Each fix has the page, the change and the reason, so nobody needs to understand AI search.

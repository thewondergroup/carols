A very happy carol service

A fundraiser that became a London Christmas tradition

Started by Charlie and Mariella Metcalfe in 2017, we've held a carol service in Notting Hill every Christmas since.

It's a fundraising event, of course, but it has also become one of the most magical carol services in London, with drinks, canapés and a host of celebrity readers and performers.

Why it matters

Blood cancer is very common

It's the UK's fifth most common cancer and the third biggest killer, claiming more lives each year than breast or prostate cancer. Lifestyle plays little part, which is partly why it's the most common cancer in children.

110People diagnosed every day
40,000People diagnosed every year
280,000Living with or in remission in the UK

But we're winning

Thirty years ago the chances of surviving a diagnosis were slim. Today most people diagnosed are much more likely to survive than die. Children surviving leukaemia:

1960
1 in 10

Today
9 in 10

Blood Cancer UK was started in 1960 by the Eastwood family, after their daughter Susan died weeks after being diagnosed. Since then it has funded over £500 million of research, with a profound effect on survival, not just in blood cancer but other cancers too.

It isn't just leukaemia

There are more than a hundred types. The most common in the UK include:

Your support

What the money does

We need to raise a lot, and every pound raised goes to Blood Cancer UK's research and support work.

£50k

Could fund a Clinical Trials Nurse for a year, helping people with blood cancer access clinical trials and supporting them before, during and after treatment.

£75k

Could maintain the nurse education programme for two years, helping nurses across the UK improve best practice in blood cancer care.

£100k

Could fund a researcher for the entirety of a research project.

£200k

Could fund an entire world-class research project, running around three years, helping scientists understand what causes blood cancer, make treatments kinder and take us closer to a cure.

£500k

Could fund Blood Cancer UK's Support Services for a year, helping people feel supported and able to access the care that's right for them.

£1m

Could fund a new Service Transformation Project, dramatically increasing the help available and the number of people reached.
